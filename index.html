Privacy Policy

Gully King: Downhill Bike Rush

Last updated: 27 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Nexsimp LLP (“we”, “us”, “our”) handles information in the mobile game Gully King: Downhill Bike Rush (the “Game”), published under our Kursor studio brand. By playing the Game, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, or contact details directly within the Game.

The Game uses third-party services that may collect limited information automatically to provide advertising, purchases, and basic performance functions:

  • Advertising identifiers and related device information (for example, a mobile advertising ID, approximate device type, and IP-based coarse location) may be collected by our advertising partner to show ads.
  • Purchase information needed to process and validate in-app purchases (a purchase token and product identifier). We do not receive or store your full payment card details; these are handled by the store and payment processors.
  • Basic technical and usage data such as crashes, device model, and operating system version, used to keep the Game working correctly.

2. Local Game Data

Your game progress (chai balance, unlocked bikes and worlds, settings, and best scores) is stored on your device. It is not uploaded to us. Removing the Game may delete this data.

3. Third-Party Services

The Game uses the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob (advertising) — https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Play Services / Play Billing (purchases, core services) — https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • RevenueCat (in-app purchase management) — https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

4. Advertising

The Game shows advertising, including optional rewarded ads (which you choose to watch) and interstitial ads. Our advertising partner may use device and advertising identifiers to show more relevant ads and to measure ad performance. You can reset or limit ad personalization at any time through your device settings (for example, “Reset advertising ID” or “Opt out of Ads Personalization” on Android). Purchasing the “Remove Ads” item disables interstitial ads within the Game.

5. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

6. Your Choices

  • Reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings.
  • Purchase “Remove Ads” to stop interstitial advertising.
  • Uninstall the Game to remove locally stored game data.

7. Data Retention

We do not maintain personal user accounts. Data processed by third-party services is retained according to their respective policies. Purchase records may be retained as required for accounting, tax, and legal purposes.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled through the Game. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.
